# Cooking quality

Status: **proposal** (the user's idea). Build it after Tongue Grab, and test it with table customers and tips in place, because without tips "worse quality" means nothing.

## Rule: quality only on "waiting" tasks

- **Heat stations** (stove now; later a grill, fryer, oven) get a quality window. You start them and walk away, so coming back at the right time is a real decision.
- **Chopping and washing stay binary.** You're already standing there working, and "over-chopped" isn't a thing.

## How it works

- The cook bar has a **green zone**.
- Green = full tip plus a sparkle on the plate.
- Slightly over = the order still counts, with a smaller tip.
- Way over = burnt, then fire, as now.
- Optional: taking food off **early** = "undercooked", the same reduced tip.
- **Perfect is a bonus, never a requirement.**

## Keeping it fun

- Wide green zone: a few seconds, not a split-second.
- Sound cues: a chime when it enters green, a sizzle warning when it leaves. Players react without staring at the bar.
- Vary by food: soups are forgiving, steak or fish later get a tight zone.

## Ties to other systems

- Tips and the VIP critic
