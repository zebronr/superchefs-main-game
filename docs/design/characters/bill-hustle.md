# Bill: Hustle

Status: **code written and smoke-tested (2026-10-05);** placeholder VFX and a stand-in character model (see HANDOFF). Job: All-rounder. Shape: **self buff**. Follows the [general skill rules](../characters.md#general-skill-rules) and the [art style](../art-style.md).

## Who Bill is

The regular human chef, and the **starter character**: everyone owns Bill before opening any crates, so he's the most common character in every lobby. His skill is the first one every new player learns.

Design goals:
- Simple: no aiming, no tricks. It teaches "you have a super skill" without a learning curve.
- Useful in every level, every team and every job.
- Never the best at anything. Specialists from crates should feel like an upgrade, but Bill never feels useless.

## Fantasy

A regular chef giving it 110%: sleeves rolled up, everything a bit faster for a few seconds.

## Use

- **Press Q.** No aim, no hold.
- For the duration, Bill gets:
  - **Faster movement**
  - **Faster chopping and washing** (less than the Octopus, which is the specialist)
- Stacks with dash. Carrying doesn't slow it.

## Numbers (start)

| Knob | Start |
|---|---|
| Duration | 5 s |
| Move speed | +30% |
| Chop / wash speed | +50% (Octopus: 2–3x) |
| Cooldown | 15 s (from when Hustle ends) |

The cooldown is shorter than the specialists' (Gecko 8 s per grab, Penguin 20 s, Alien 25 s) because the effect is smaller. Bill gets to use his skill often, so new players use it often and learn it fast.

## Interrupt

Overrides the general rule: Hustle is a self buff with nothing to aim, so a thrown item doesn't cancel it.

## VFX (art style: squishy, elastic)

- **Start:** Bill squashes down and springs up, with a puff of steam from his head (a "let's go" moment).
- **During:** small motion lines and a light trail behind him, sweat drops flicking off every so often, and the knife or sponge moves visibly faster.
- **End:** a short exhale puff, so the player knows it's over.
- **Sound:** a quick "hup!" or whoosh on start, a faster footstep rhythm during, and a breath out at the end.

## Playtest questions
