# Filipino content

Status: **proposal** (2026-10-07). The user is Filipino. Filipino players hype up Filipino things, and Filipino TikTok and Facebook gaming groups are huge, so this is identity *and* free marketing.

Rule: **a celebration, not a joke about Filipinos.** Things Filipinos would be proud to share. Run it past a few Filipino friends before release.

## Ideas, cheapest first

### 1. Filipino recipes (cheap, high impact)

Fits the plate and recipe system that exists.
- **Silog breakfasts:** tapsilog, longsilog, tocilog. Tapa + sinangag + itlog = a combo-style naming pattern the plate system already does.
- **Halo-halo:** an assembly dish with many ingredients. The ultimate plating challenge.
- **Lumpia, pancit, adobo, sinigang, turon.**
- **Sisig on a sizzling plate:** a heat station with its own sizzle.
- **Lechon:** a spit-roast station you turn (later).

### 2. Taglish customer lines (cheap)

Speech bubbles and voice barks:
- Ordering: "Pabili po!"
- Paying (table cash): "Bayad po!"
- Leaving happy: "Salamat po!"
- Dine-and-dasher: "Takbo!" (Run!)
- Angry: "Ang tagal naman!" (What's taking so long!)

Roblox auto-translation won't touch these if they're the joke, so keep them few and obvious.

### 3. Aling Inasal (the user's restaurant, 2026-10-07)

A parody of the grilled-chicken-and-unli-rice chain every Filipino knows. It's the restaurant inside the Filipino map.
- **Chicken inasal on a grill:** a heat station, so it gets a perfect-cook window later.
- **Unli-rice refills:** [follow-up orders](follow-up-orders.md), with a rice bucket server walking the tables.
- Keep the parody obvious but original: its own logo, mascot and colours, not the real chain's. Roblox moderation and the real brand both care about that.
- In December it's decorated for Pasko (below); off-season the decorations come off and it's a regular map.

### 4. A Filipino map: "Pasko sa Barrio" (the December map)

Aling Inasal in a barrio, decorated for a Filipino Christmas: parol lanterns, string lights everywhere, a belen, karaoke in the corner, kids caroling at the door. Filipinos have the longest Christmas season in the world, so a December release is perfect.

**Signature mechanic: Brownout.** Every so often the power goes out:
- Lights dim (candles and parols still glow).
- Electric stoves and the rice cooker stop; the gas stove still works.
- Power comes back after a few seconds, or faster if someone flips the breaker.

Every Filipino knows the brownout. It's relatable, funny, and a real kitchen mechanic.

Proposal: this **replaces the Winter Lodge** as the December map (Winter Lodge moves to a later update). It's still a Christmas map for everyone, but nobody else has it.

| Level | Idea |
|---|---|
| Simbang Gabi | Early-morning rush after church: puto bumbong and bibingka. |
| Fiesta | A crowded street party, lots of customers. |
| Brownout | The signature mechanic. |
| Noche Buena | The midnight feast finale: big orders, everything on. |

### 5. A folklore character (post-beta)

The roster already has a Gecko, a Ghost and an Alien, so Filipino folklore fits right in. Cartoon and cute, not scary.

| Creature | Superskill idea |
|---|---|
| **Manananggal** | **Split:** the upper body flies off to serve or fetch while the legs stay at the station and keep chopping. A two-places-at-once skill nobody else has. |
| **Tikbalang** | **Gallop:** a long, fast charge in a straight line; in Versus it leaves enemies dizzy. |
| **Duwende** | **Tiny:** shrinks and runs under counters and tables as shortcuts. |
| **Kapre** | **Giant reach:** grabs and places items over counters from far away. |

Best fit: the Manananggal. The mechanic is unique and very clip-able.
