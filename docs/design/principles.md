# Design principles

Check every new idea against these.

## Goal

Stop being "Overcooked on Roblox". Overcooked is built for 2–4 friends on a couch. Roblox players are mostly strangers in short sessions who come back for progress, flexing and chaos.

## Depth, not complexity

1. **Connect.** A new mechanic should interact with existing ones. If it touches nothing, cut it or change it.
2. **Small floor, high ceiling.** The floor is what everyone must do (grab, chop, cook, plate, serve), and a stranger should get it in one round. Put almost every new idea on the ceiling: optional mastery that bad players can ignore.
3. **Mechanic budget per level:** the core loop plus about 2 featured mechanics. The game has lots of depth overall, but each round stays readable.
4. **One new thing at a time.** Each chapter or level introduces one mechanic before stacking it with others.
5. **Readability.** Every mechanic needs a clear visual, a sound cue, and an instant reason when something fails ("Undercooked! -2 tip").
6. **Cut.** Add one thing, playtest, and ask: did anyone use it, and was it fun? Keep the best 60%.
7. **"Waiting" tasks can have timing quality; "doing" tasks stay binary.**
