# TikTok edit

A vertical (9:16), beat-synced edit of the demo for TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Reels and r/roblox. Part of the [demo launch](demo-launch.md).

Record only after the checklist's VFX and models are real: a zoom punch on a placeholder effect makes it look *more* unfinished.

## The hook

Turn last year's flame into the story:

> **"Last year Reddit called my game an Overcooked ripoff..."**
> *(beat drop)*
> **"...so I gave every chef superpowers."**

"Got roasted, came back better" is a story people root for, and making the joke first takes the ripoff comment off the table.

| 12–15 s | Chaos montage: 4 players, fire, throwing, tongue catches | One cut per beat |
| 15–18 s | Character lineup: Gecko, Bill, Penguin, plus a silhouette ("more coming...") | Each pops in on a beat |
| 18–20 s | Title card plus "Demo out now" | Hold on the logo |

## Edit techniques

- **Cut on the beat.** CapCut's auto beat detection lines superskills up with hits.
- **Zoom punch and shake** on impacts (tongue hit, Penguin stomp).
- **Speed ramps:** slow right before a superskill lands, then snap back.
- **Freeze frame with text** on the best moment ("NICE CATCH").
- **Big bold captions** for story beats; many people watch muted.

## Recording in Roblox

- Hide the UI for cinematic shots. Record at 60 fps or higher so clips can be slowed down cleanly.
- Get friends or alt accounts to play so the kitchen looks busy.
- Record many takes of each superskill moment. One perfect tongue catch might take 20 tries.
- Use a Studio camera script for smooth sweeping kitchen shots instead of shaky player-camera footage.

## Music

Use **royalty-free or licensed** music, not a trending copyrighted song. On TikTok, the commercial sound library is safe. Reposts to Reddit or YouTube with copyrighted music can get muted or taken down.
